# Review Priority Guidelines

Demo internal policy for the Enterprise AI Operations Agent portfolio project.
Application-owned guidance, not an official Olist policy.

## High priority

Mark an investigation urgent or high only for an explicitly reported material
concern needing prompt human review, such as a well-described significant recorded
payment discrepancy with supporting evidence. High priority is a triage request,
not proof of fraud, an automatic refund, or permission to bypass approval. This
demo has no invented monetary threshold or guaranteed response-time SLA.

## Medium priority

Use medium for routine internal review with a defined concern but no supported
urgency. A clear review request with no priority supplied may use medium as a
communicated neutral default. Do not fabricate severity. Ask for clarification
when action intent or urgency is ambiguous. The reason may state the user's review
intent if no specific concern was supplied.

## Low priority

Use low for informational follow-up or a nonurgent clarification with no evidence
of an immediate business consequence. Preserve the stated concern. Low priority
still requires human approval before the internal review case is created, and does
not authorize automated external action.

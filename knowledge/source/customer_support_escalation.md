# Customer Support Escalation

Demo internal policy for the Enterprise AI Operations Agent portfolio project.
Application-owned guidance, not an official Olist policy.

## Refund execution unavailable

This system cannot issue, approve, process or settle refunds. An internal order
review is an investigation request, not a refund action. Refuse unsupported refund
execution clearly; do not claim money was returned or substitute a review action
without a user's explicit request for internal review. Recorded payment differences
do not establish refund eligibility or a confirmed financial error.

## Historical customer contact boundary

Olist customer records are historical and anonymized. They cannot support real
customer contact, a current customer identity, or a current support conversation.
The system has no email, messaging or customer-contact integration. Do not infer
contact details, claim a person was notified, or make current inventory decisions
from this snapshot. customer_unique_id links historical repeat-customer records;
customer_id identifies the order-associated customer record.

## Internal support escalation

Prepare a concise human-review note with the historical order reference, the
user's qualified concern and available read-only evidence. Do not expose unrelated
customer data. If explicitly requested, propose the existing internal order-review
case; it requires a separately authorized approval before execution. No external
support ticket or notification is created by this guidance.

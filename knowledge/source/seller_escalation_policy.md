# Seller Escalation Policy

Demo internal policy for the Enterprise AI Operations Agent portfolio project.
Application-owned guidance, not an official Olist policy.

## Preparing seller escalation

For a seller-related concern, first identify the order and relevant item/seller
references through read-only data tools. Summarize the concern, supporting evidence,
and missing information for a human operator. If an internal order review is
requested, propose that existing capability with an appropriate reason and priority.
An escalation note is guidance, not a separately implemented action or dispatch queue.

## Evidence limitations

shipping_limit_date is a seller handoff deadline, not an actual shipping timestamp.
Historical dates alone do not establish present seller performance, inventory,
payout liability or a currently actionable delivery incident. Clearly separate
recorded observations from assumptions and do not invent operational status.

## No seller dispatch

The demo has no seller messaging, payout, notification or inventory integration.
Never claim a seller was contacted, a payout was changed, or stock was reserved.
Any real escalation would require verified current records, authorized human
operators and a future external integration; none is implemented here.
